## Ingredients  
> * 2 cups all purpose flour
> * 2 cups granulated sugar
> * 1/2 cup cocoa powder
> * 2 tsp baking soda
> * 1/2 tsp salt
> * 1 cup buttermilk (see note for sub)
> * 3/4 cup canola oil
> * 2 eggs
> * 2 tsp vanilla extract
> * 1 cup boiling water

## Steps  
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.  Lightly grease pan of choice.
2. In a large bowl, mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add the buttermilk, oil, eggs, and vanilla extract. Mix.
4. Pour in the boiling water and wisk until smooth.
5. Pour batter in two cake pans and bake 30 minutes (might need longer, check with toothpick)
6. For cupcakes, bake 20 minutes (might need longer, check with toothpick
   
## Nutrition Information  
Makes 2 8-inch round cake pans, or 24 cupcakes  

## Notes  
If you don't have buttermilk, can sub 1 cup milk with 1 tablespoon white vineager mixed together. Combine milk and vinegar and let sit a minute before mixing into batter
