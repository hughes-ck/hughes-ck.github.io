## Ingredients  
> * 1 lb ground beef
> * 1 can (48 oz) tomato juice
> * 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
> * 1/2 medium onion, chopped
> * 1/4 cup sugar
> * 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
> * 1/2 teaspoon salt
> * 1/4 teaspoon pepper
> * 1/2 lb mostaccioli noodles
> * 8 oz rope-style provel cheese

## Steps  
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F.  Lightly grease 9x13 pan; set aside.
2. Crumble ground beef into large saucepan; cook over medium heat stirring occasionally until meat is browned. Drain off fat.
3. Add remaining ingredients except pasta and cheese; bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for at least 15 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, in a large pot of boiling water, cook mostaccioli according to package directions, then drain well.
5. Add pasta to sauce and stir to mix.
6. Place pasta and sauce in prepared baking dish. Sprinkle cheese over top.
7. Bake in 375 degree F oven for 12 to 15 minutes or until cheese is melted and lightly browned.
   
## Nutrition Information  
Makes 8 servings
